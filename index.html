APK Sharing & App Backup privacy policy

Applies to APK Sharing & App Backup (package app.shareapps.apkextractor) on Google Play and Galaxy Store. Last updated 9 October 2026.

APK Sharing & App Backup ("the app") is published by Zoopet ("we"). This policy explains what the app does with information on your device. Contact: contact@zoopet.in.

The short version

  • No account, no sign-in, and no servers of our own. We do not receive your app list, your files or your backups.
  • Files and links leave your phone only when you choose a destination in Android's share sheet or a folder in Android's folder picker.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob and Unity Ads, which process device and advertising identifiers as described below.

Advertising

Unless you buy "Remove ads", the app shows ads using Google Mobile Ads (AdMob), which may deliver ads from Unity Ads through mediation. These services may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), IP address, approximate location derived from IP, app interactions with ads, and diagnostic data, for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. In the UK, EEA and Switzerland, and where required elsewhere, we ask for your consent through Google's consent tool before ads are requested; you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings.

Purchases

Payments are handled entirely by Google Play or Samsung Galaxy Store. We never see your payment details. The app receives only a purchase record to confirm that ads should be removed.

Permissions

The app requests Internet and network-state access only, for ads and purchases. It does not request storage, phone, contacts, location or "install unknown apps" permissions. Android limits which installed apps it can see to apps with a home-screen entry, home apps, keyboards and live wallpapers.

Sharing apps responsibly

Only share apps you have the right to share. For paid apps, send the store link instead of the file.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and is not designed for the Google Play Families programme.

Retention and deletion

Everything the app stores is on your device. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it deletes its settings and history. Backup files you saved in Download/APK Backups or another folder remain until you delete them, for example from the Backups tab.

Your rights

Because we do not hold personal data about you, there is usually nothing for us to access, correct or delete. For questions about data processed by Google or Unity for ads, use the links above. Contact us at contact@zoopet.in for anything else.

Changes

We will update this page if the app's data handling changes, and change the date at the top.
